## Numerical claims (reviewer metadata — NOT attendee copy, design §7.2)

The AAO-Macquarie MAVIS material currently supports figures such as **6–8 laser
guide stars** and about **15 milli-arcsecond** imaging performance. These are
kept out of the attendee-facing fact bodies deliberately, so the learning moment
stays short and does not depend on instrument-design numbers that may be revised.
