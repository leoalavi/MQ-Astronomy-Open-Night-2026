Astronomy Open Night — Privacy Policy

Astronomy Open Night is published on Google Play by Leo Alavi, an independent developer. Privacy contact: leo@leoalavi.dev. Last updated: 5 September 2026.

There is no account, sign-in, advertising or developer-operated analytics service. Passport stamps, favourites, your saved plan and preferences are stored locally. Android may include local data in system backups or device transfers according to your device settings.

Location is optional. It is processed on your device for the campus map and compass. When you request walking directions and agree to Google Maps, the route origin (which may be your precise or approximate location) and destination are sent to Google over HTTPS.

Google Maps also receives map requests, IP address, device and app information, an SDK-specific identifier, crash diagnostics and map interactions to provide and improve its services. No Google map is loaded until you agree; you can revoke that choice in Settings.

On Android, the QR scanner uses Google ML Kit. Camera images are processed on your device and are not saved or uploaded by the app. ML Kit reports device/app information, installation identifiers, performance and usage diagnostics to Google over HTTPS. You can enter the printed station code instead of using the camera.

Saved stamps, favourites and plans remain until you delete them. Settings → Delete my data clears these items and Google Maps consent; language and appearance preferences are retained. This does not delete data already received by Google or copies held in system backups. Google controls retention and deletion of its service data under its privacy policy, linked below.

We do not operate a server holding your saved plan or stamps. Local storage uses Android's app sandbox; this does not promise absolute security. Contact the address above with privacy questions. This policy covers all visitors.
